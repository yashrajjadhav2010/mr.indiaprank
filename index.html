Methodology & Scoring Rubric

The MR INDIA Student Ranking Lab utilizes a multi-dimensional psychometric model calibrated across 15 behavioral vectors.

  • Vector Calibration: Evaluates task prioritization, cognitive chronobiology, conceptual retention, and social alignment.
  • Normal Distribution Benchmarking: Scores are mapped against a simulated normalized bell-curve cohort (N = 1,480) with a standard deviation of 12.4.
  • Archetype Classification: Synthesizes primary cognitive tendencies into descriptive behavioral profiles with tailored heuristics.

* Designed for non-invasive self-assessment and academic reflection. No diagnostic medical claims are made.

Competency Framework

Our evaluation matrix covers five fundamental student performance dimensions:

  • Analytical Acumen: First-principles conceptual clarity and logical problem deduction.
  • Intellectual Curiosity: Deep learning drive and cognitive appetite for complex subjects.
  • Execution Agility: Adaptability under tight deadlines and changing project scopes.
  • Creative Synthesis: Lateral ideation and interdisciplinary connection-making.
  • Peer Collaboration: Group resonance, empathy, and constructive communication.

Research Scope & Transparency

MR INDIA — Student Ranking Lab operates as an independent academic diagnostic exercise.

The platform is structured to demonstrate modern interactive assessment design, behavioral vector mapping, and instant client-side reporting without requiring server-side state tracking.

Privacy & Data Ethics Standards

All responses are computed exclusively within your local browser environment.

This application enforces strict zero-retention privacy protocols:

  • No user accounts, passwords, or authentication credentials required.
  • No personal identification data (phone, email, school IDs, or financial records) collected.
  • Zero tracking cookies or cross-site telemetry trackers.
  • Local assessment state is cleared upon session termination or page reset.
